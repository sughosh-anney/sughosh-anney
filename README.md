## About Me

I hold a **dual Finance + IT mandate** at ADF Foods Ltd, reporting directly to the CFO. One half of the role is classic FP&A — P&L, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow reporting, quarterly forecasting and budgeting across every SKU, and actual-vs-forecast variance analysis reviewed with Country Managers. The other half is engineering: I lead the IT function, design and maintain the databases behind it, and build the dashboards and applications the CFO, CEO, Directors and Country Managers use day to day.

The practical consequence is that I do not hand a spec to someone else. I define the metric, model it, write the pipeline, build the interface, and own it in production.

- **Currently:** Assistant Manager – IT Finance & FP&A at ADF Foods Ltd, Mumbai (Jul 2026 – Present), reporting to the CFO
- **Finance:** FP&A ownership, MIS reporting, SKU-level forecasting, multi-entity consolidation, working capital analytics
- **IT:** Leading the IT function — SQL databases, ERP integration, internal web applications, dashboard delivery and hosting
- **In flight:** Leading the enterprise ERP migration from Orion to SAP S/4HANA, currently in UAT — user training, consultant onboarding, finance-side migration readiness
- **Recognition:** Deloitte AI Integrated Award 2025 | 51 AI automation workflows deployed
- **Published:** 2 International Research Papers | 3x Conference Speaker
